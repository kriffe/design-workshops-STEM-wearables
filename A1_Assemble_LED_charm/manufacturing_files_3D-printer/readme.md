## How to manufacture LED charm with 3D-printer

The LED charm has two parts, base and lid, that can be printed on a basic [FFF printer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fused_filament_fabrication) with a nozzle of 0.4 mm or 0.8 mm (size of the extruded plastic). Support material is usually not needed for this type of design.

Recommended material is a transparent and colorless thermoplastic like [PLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3D_printing_filament) so the LED light easily shows through both body and lid.
